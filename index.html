Frequently asked questions

A few things you may want to know.

Do I need to know Maqamaat before joining?

No. There are learning options for those starting with the fundamentals as well as students looking to develop further.

Will I still follow Tajweed?

Yes. Maqamaat is taught in the context of Qur’an recitation, with Tajweed remaining essential.

How long does the course take?

The programme lasts 18 months. You’ll stay with the same dedicated learning group, developing your recitation and progressing together.

How does the class work?

You’ll learn with the same dedicated group throughout the programme, building your skills together through online lessons and shared activities.

Is the course online?

Yes. You can learn online from wherever you are.

What learning options are available?

We offer recorded lessons, a monthly learning option and 1-to-1 mentoring. You can explore the available options through the questionnaire above.

Can I learn at my own pace?

The recorded lessons let you study at your own pace. Other learning options offer additional guidance and support.
