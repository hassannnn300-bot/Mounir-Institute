The Mounir Method

A clear path from learning to living it.

Build your recitation step by step. Each stage gives you a practical skill to understand, practise and bring into your Qur’an recitation.

01
Stage one · Understand

Start with the foundations

Understand what Maqamaat are, how they shape the feel of a recitation, and how to approach them with care while keeping the Qur’an and correct Tajweed at the centre.

Know what you’re learning and why
02
Stage two · Hear it

Train your ear and voice

Learn to recognise a Maqam, follow its basic melodic movement, and practise it through guided listening, simple scales and humming exercises.

Recognise the sound before applying it
03
Stage three · Apply

Put it into Surah Al-Fatiha

Move from exercises into recitation. Use Maqam Nahawand as a practical starting point and learn how to apply what you hear to familiar verses.

Connect practice to real recitation
04
Stage four · Build control

Develop your breath and vocal technique

Use focused breathing techniques and vocal drills to support steadier phrases, greater control and a more comfortable practice routine.

Build control through consistent practice
05
Stage five · Expand

Explore all eight Maqamaat

Work through the eight Maqamaat in a structured sequence, learning to recognise the character of each one and practise its movement at your own pace.

Grow your range one Maqam at a time
06
Stage six · Refine

Practise, review and make it your own

Bring the skills together in regular recitation practice. Revisit lessons, strengthen the areas that need work and use mentoring where you want more personalised guidance.

Turn lessons into a lasting practice

Progress at a steady pace. The aim is not to rush through stages, but to build understanding and apply each skill with care.
