Learn with Hassan Mounir

A method shaped by years of learning.

With more than 800,000 followers across social media, Hassan Mounir has travelled internationally to learn from respected teachers in Egypt, Morocco, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan. Through this journey, he has studied the art of Maqamaat and the different styles of Qur’anic recitation, bringing those experiences into a clear, structured way to learn.

At Mounir Institute, the aim is to help students understand the Maqamaat, train their ear and voice, and apply what they learn thoughtfully in their own Qur’an recitation.

  • Learn from a method shaped by study across four countries.
  • Explore Maqamaat in the context of Qur’anic recitation—as a studied recitation tradition.
  • Develop through guided lessons, vocal drills and breathing techniques.
